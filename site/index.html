Shanghai

The Aman on one side, Wukang Mansion on the other.

Split view puts two pages in one window: the hotel, and the walk down Huaihai Road you’re planning from it.

New York

Look it up without leaving Katz’s.

Shift-click a link and it opens over the page you’re on. Read about the pastrami on rye, then press Escape and carry on.

Already open

Type katz. It’s the tab you already have.

Press Command-T and start typing. Zigzag finds the tab before it opens a new one, so the same page never turns up twice.

The whole weekend

Every tab, laid out like a contact sheet.

Shift-Command-Backslash shows every space at once. Pick the one you meant, or clear out a few.

Studio

The Bauhaus, set like a magazine.

Reader keeps the words and the pictures and drops everything else: big type, a lead photograph, pictures in the margin.

Passwords
Kept in your Mac’s Keychain and filled in with a click. A strong one for every new account.
Extensions
Add them from the Chrome Web Store. Zigzag shows what each one can see first.
Blocking
Ads and trackers stopped as pages load. Turn it off for a site you want to support.
Private windows
No history, no cookies, no extensions. Close the window and it’s gone.
Translate
Pages translated on your Mac with Apple’s translation. The text never leaves it.
Switching
Bring bookmarks, history and passwords from Arc, Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Brave, Dia, Vivaldi or Opera. Arc spaces come over as they were.
