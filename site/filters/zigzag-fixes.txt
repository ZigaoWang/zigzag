! Title: Zigzag fixes
! Description: Fixes for sites that Zigzag's ad and tracker blocking gets in the way of or misses, in uBlock Origin's syntax.
! Zigzag downloads this list with uBlock Origin's own and runs it through the same engine and scriptlets.
! Homepage: https://zigzagbrowser.com
! Expires: 12 hours
!
! To add a fix: one line here, then deploy the site. Each Zigzag has it within 12 hours, with no new release.
! Keep each entry as narrow as the block it works around, with its issue above it. More in Zigzag/Web/AdFilters.swift.

! DeepSeek posts its telemetry to /api/v0/telemetry and EasyPrivacy blocks it (||chat.deepseek.com/api/v0/telemetry).
! After three failures in a row, while its other calls go through, it says "Please check your browser extensions".
! Its fetch and XMLHttpRequest get an empty answer instead: nothing is sent, as with the block.
! https://github.com/ZigaoWang/zigzag/issues/208
chat.deepseek.com##+js(prevent-fetch, /api/v0/telemetry)
chat.deepseek.com##+js(prevent-xhr, /api/v0/telemetry)

! HLTV (hltv.org) places its betting ads itself: pictures from its own /img/ path, links through /out2/, the two
! columns beside the page, an odds box by the logo, and a clickable picture behind the page set on <body> by its
! scripts. uBlock Origin stops their pictures (||hltv.org/img/*?ixlib=$image), a request rule Zigzag's content blocker
! doesn't have, so they're hidden here, by the places they stand in, not the names its scripts make up each build.
! https://github.com/ZigaoWang/zigzag/issues/283
hltv.org##.bg-sidebar
hltv.org##.fbw-flex-wrapper
hltv.org##div:has(> a[href^="/out2/"] > img)
hltv.org##aside:has(> a[href^="/out2/"])
hltv.org##aside:has(> div > a[href^="/out2/"])
hltv.org##+js(ra, style|data-href, body, stay)
